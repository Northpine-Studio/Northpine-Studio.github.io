DayCast Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

DayCast (“the app”) is a weather app for locations in the United States, published by Northpine Apps (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • DayCast has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not run our own servers for the app and we do not receive or store your personal information.
  • DayCast does not ask for location permission and does not read your device’s GPS location. You choose the places you want weather for.
  • To show weather, the app sends the coordinates of the place you selected to the US National Weather Service. To find places, it sends your search text to the US Census Bureau.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, IP address and ad interaction and diagnostic data, as described in section 4.
  • DayCast does not read your contacts, calendar, photos, files, or health data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • Your saved and favorite locations (place name, state and coordinates).
  • The most recent weather data, so it can be shown when you are offline and on the DayCast home screen widget.
  • Your unit settings (for example Fahrenheit or Celsius).

This information is not sent to us. You can delete it by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Weather and place search services

National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). To get forecasts, current conditions and alerts, the app sends the latitude and longitude of the place you selected (rounded to 4 decimal places) to the National Weather Service, a US government agency. Requests are made over HTTPS. If you enter coordinates manually, those coordinates are sent. The National Weather Service’s own privacy practices apply to these requests: https://www.weather.gov/privacy

US Census Bureau TIGERweb (tigerweb.geo.census.gov). When you search for a city, town or ZIP code, the text you type is sent to the Census Bureau’s public geographic service over HTTPS to find matching places. Your coordinates are not sent with searches. See https://www.census.gov/about/policies/privacy.html

Like any internet request, these services receive your device’s IP address. We do not receive these requests and we do not combine them with any other information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of DayCast shows ads provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Product interactions: for example app launches, taps and ad views.
  • Diagnostic information: for example app launch time and SDK performance.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google’s use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google’s privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

DayCast does not send ad partners your selected or saved locations, your searches, or weather content, and it does not use location for ad targeting.

Your choices:

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and certain US states), the app asks for your consent or gives you choices through Google’s consent tool before ads are requested. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices when that option is shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).

5. Analytics and crash reports

DayCast does not include a third-party analytics service, and crash reporting is not currently enabled. If we add crash reporting or analytics in a future version, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe what is collected.

6. Children

DayCast is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the services described in sections 3 and 4, the app does not share information with third parties.

8. Security

All network requests made by the app use HTTPS. Information stored on your device is kept in the app’s private storage.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
