Frequently asked questions

Where does the weather data come from?

Forecasts, current conditions and weather alerts come from the US National Weather Service (NWS). DayCast covers locations in the United States.

Why does a value show “—”?

When the weather service does not report a value (for example, a station temporarily has no temperature reading), DayCast shows “—” instead of guessing.

Does DayCast need my location?

No. DayCast does not ask for location permission. Search for a US city or ZIP code to choose the places you want weather for.

How many days of forecast are shown?

DayCast shows the days the National Weather Service actually provides — usually about 7 days. It never fills in extra days.

Is weather radar available?

Not yet. Radar will only be added once a properly licensed, real radar data source is in place.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy in the app. Where your region requires it, an Ad privacy choices option lets you review or change your choices. See the privacy policy for details.
